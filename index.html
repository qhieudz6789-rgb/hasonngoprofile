About

I am a power systems engineer with a Ph.D., currently working on grid interconnection studies, inverter-based resource (IBR) modeling, and protection engineering for utility-scale projects. My day-to-day spans PSS/E dynamic simulations, BESS integration, relay settings, and harmonic analysis — mostly within the ERCOT framework.

This site is my learning journal: a place to document what I am figuring out, share useful frameworks, and connect with others working on hard problems in the energy and data infrastructure space.

Overview

Research Background

Quang-Ha Ngo received his B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from Hanoi University of Science and Technology in 2021 and his M.S. from Clarkson University in 2023. He is currently pursuing a Ph.D. at the Center for Electric Power System Research (CEPSR), Clarkson University, Potsdam, NY. He also served as a Distribution Grid Resilience Intern at ABB during the Summer and Fall of 2025. His research interests include smart grids, naval ship power systems, distribution power systems, microgrids, and deep learning algorithms.

Positions

Research Experience

Graduate Research Assistant 06/2022 – present

Smart Power Systems and Controls Lab, Electrical and Computer Engineering Department, Clarkson University, Potsdam, New York, USA

  • Contributing to a National Science Foundation (NSF)-funded project titled "Physics-informed Graph Learning for State Estimation and Anomaly Detection in Power Systems," developing a distributed learning technique for state estimation in large-scale power transmission and distribution systems, including a novel method to enhance state estimation and anomaly detection for power system operators using graph-based and physics-informed techniques.
  • Contributing to an Office of Naval Research (ONR)-funded project titled "Integrated Intelligent Cyber-Physical Fault-Containment Control for Resilient PEPDS," developing a novel deep graph neural network for detecting and managing cyber-physical fault events on the shipboard system, along with a documented vulnerability assessment of pole-to-pole faults in the MVDC shipboard system.
  • Collaborating on a New York State Energy Research and Development Authority (NYSERDA)-funded project titled "Coordinated Grid Controllers and DERs to Enhance Distribution Grid Operation," reviewing and designing coordinated control of grid controllers and smart inverters for reliable, stable distribution grid operation with large-scale distributed energy resources, and developing benchmark distribution system models with integrated PV units for long-term dynamic analysis of voltage violation scenarios.
  • Participating in a Clarkson-sponsored project titled "Green-Energy Powered Autonomous Driving Pod (ADP) On-Campus Demonstration," designing a combined solar and wind powered charging station for autonomous vehicles and a hardware-in-the-loop integration for grid impact studies.
  • Set up a cyber communication network using ns-3 and collaborated with colleagues to integrate a load-sharing controller and a real-time simulator, evaluating the combined impact of physical faults and cyber attacks (Denial of Service, Man-in-the-Middle, and False Data Injection Attacks) on hardware-in-the-loop (HIL) testbeds.
